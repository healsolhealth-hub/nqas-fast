In the app

  1. Sign in to NQAS | FAST (Google Play app or app.thehealsol.com).

Can't sign in? Use the form below, or write to info@thehealsol.com / WhatsApp +91 83840 63112 from your registered mobile number.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted within 30 days: your login, and your personal details — name, mobile number and email address — and your name on cards, actions and comments.
  • Kept, without your name: the facility's programme records (checklists, scores, actions, evidence). They belong to the facility's NQAS work and stay with the facility; they are kept for up to 3 years after the programme cycle ends, matching the NQAS certification period.
  • Kept as the law requires: payment and invoice records (for tax purposes, normally 8 years).

We confirm on your mobile number when it is done. See the Privacy Policy.
