About NQAS | FAST & Pricing

NQAS | FAST is an online quality-improvement programme by HealSol Academy (HEALSOL HEALTHCARE PRIVATE LIMITED). It helps Sub-Centres / Ayushman Arogya Mandirs, UPHCs, PHCs and CHCs prepare for National Quality Assurance Standards (NQAS) assessment through daily task cards, checklists, evidence upload, review by supervisors, progress reports and certificates, in English and Hindi, on phone and computer.

Packages (per facility)

  • No-cost baseline assessment — for every newly registered facility: 7 days for Sub-Centres, UPHCs and PHCs; 14 days for CHCs. ₹0.
  • Sub-Centre 12A — 12 Services programme, 60 days: ₹3,499
  • Sub-Centre 12B — 12 Services + Delivery programme, 60 days: ₹3,999
  • UPHC, PHC and CHC packages — the price of each package is shown in the app before payment.
  • District, State, NGO and sponsored enrolments — as per quotation.

Prices are in Indian Rupees. GST, where applicable, is shown separately at checkout before you pay. Launch offers, when running, are announced in the app and apply only as stated.

To start: open app.thehealsol.com → Join NQAS FAST.

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · prepared with reference to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

1. What we collect

  • About you: name, mobile number, email (if given), role / designation, and the facility, block, district and state you work in.
  • About your facility: name, NIN, type, location, linked facilities, population served, and your programme work: checklists, scores, actions, review comments, photos and documents uploaded as evidence.
  • Payments: package, amount, date and payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our payment partner Razorpay; we do not store them.
  • Technical: login times, device and browser type, and error logs, to keep the app secure and working.

2. Why we use it

  • To approve registrations, create logins and provide the programme.
  • To show progress to the people your role links you with (for example your facility team, Supervisor, MO / MOIC, District Admin, or a State / sponsor dashboard for facilities in their area).
  • To send reminders and service messages (in the app, by notification, SMS, WhatsApp or email).
  • To issue certificates and recognition, and to report programme impact in aggregate.
  • To process payments, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Consent

By registering, you consent to this processing. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us (see Contact); we will then close your login. Programme records of your facility may be kept as described below.

4. Public information

If your facility earns recognition (for example an impact card or certificate), the facility name, district, state and achievement may appear on a public verification page. HealSol can hide facility names for a state on request. Personal mobile numbers are never shown publicly.

5. Where data is stored and who processes it

Data is stored on Google Cloud / Firebase. Payments are processed by Razorpay. Messages may be sent through WhatsApp or SMS providers. These service providers process data only on our instructions and under their security commitments.

6. Security

Access is controlled by role-based rules, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secured logins. No system is fully secure; tell us at once if you suspect a problem.

7. How long we keep data

We keep personal data while your login is active and for up to 3 years after the programme cycle ends (matching the NQAS certification period), unless the law requires longer. Aggregate, anonymised statistics may be kept longer.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and raise a grievance. Write to the Grievance Officer below; we respond within 30 days.

In the app: Me → Delete my account. Without the app: app.thehealsol.com/delete-account.html.
